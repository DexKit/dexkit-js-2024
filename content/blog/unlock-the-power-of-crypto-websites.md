## Why build your own crypto website?

In the spirit of transparency and fairness, **we can confidently conclude that OpenSea and Rarible are ideal platforms for newcomers to the world of digital collectibles.** These platforms have invested considerable time in refining their products and have earned genuine recognition for their quality.

**Our product, on the other hand, is built for entrepreneurs and brands who are ready for the next step**—people who already tried other marketplaces and hit limitations around:

- Supported networks and tokens  
- Fee structures and revenue sharing  
- Deep UI/UX customization  
- Full control over branding, domain, and data

If you’ve been exploring [NFT business models](https://dexkit.com/blog/revolutionize-your-nft-sales-with-dexkit/) or want to own the full funnel around your community, a white‑label marketplace is the logical next move.
