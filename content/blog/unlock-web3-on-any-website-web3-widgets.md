The future of the web is **interoperable** — and with DexKit’s **Web3 Widgets**, you can bring powerful blockchain features to *any* website or platform without rebuilding your tech stack.

Whether your platform is in Web2 or Web3, live in production or just an idea on a whiteboard, Web3 Widgets let you **embed token swaps, NFT mints, token drops, and more** directly into your site with zero hassle.

---

## What Are Web3 Widgets?

Web3 Widgets are **plug-and-play blockchain components** powered by DexKit’s no-code/low-code Builder.  
Instead of writing smart contract integrations from scratch, you can embed ready-made components using a simple **iframe** — just copy, paste, and you’re live.

**Supported components include:**
- 🔄 **Swap** – Aggregate liquidity from multiple DEXs to offer the best prices.
- 🎨 **NFT Minting** – Let your users mint NFTs directly on your site.
- 🎁 **Token Drops** – Distribute ERC20 tokens with built-in claim logic.
- 🛒 **Marketplace Features** – Buy, sell, or trade assets seamlessly.

## How Easy Is It?

Here’s all it takes to get started:

1. **Open the Builder** → [dexappbuilder.dexkit.com](https://dexappbuilder.dexkit.com)
2. **Connect Your Wallet** → Authenticate in seconds.
3. **Create a Widget** → Choose the component you want (Swap, NFT Mint, etc.).
4. **Customize** → Select network, tokens, theme, and fees.
5. **Embed** → Copy your iframe code and paste it into your site.

Example:
```html
<iframe src="https://dexappbuilder.dexkit.com/_widget_iframe?widgetId=1" frameBorder="0" width="1000px" height="500px"></iframe>
```

That’s it — your Web3 component is live.
