### What is a swap?

A swap is a **transaction between two parties where they exchange assets or financial commitments over a specified period of time**. This exchange can involve various assets, including currencies, stocks, commodities, and, of course, cryptocurrencies.

In the world of cryptocurrencies, crypto swaps typically involve exchanging one crypto for another at a set value. For example, someone may swap Bitcoin for Ethereum or vice versa. People usually do this for several reasons:

- **Diversify their portfolio**
- **Seize arbitrage opportunities**, buying low in one market and selling high in another
- **Speculate on prices**, betting on moves up or down

### Types of crypto swaps

There are different types of swap operations in the crypto world, each with its own features and purposes:

**1\. Peer-to-Peer (P2P) swaps:** These occur **directly between users without an intermediary custodian**. Users can execute crypto swaps through smart contracts on blockchains like Ethereum or on decentralized exchanges (DEXs). To illustrate, let’s compare these two options:

* **Smart contracts on blockchains:** Self-executing programs stored on chains like Ethereum. They define the conditions of a transaction and automatically execute when they are met. In P2P swaps, smart contracts facilitate direct exchanges between users and ensure that both parties receive the agreed assets or the trade is reverted.
* **Decentralized exchanges (DEXs):** Trading platforms that function in a decentralized way. There’s no central authority controlling transactions; everything is handled by smart contracts. **Users trade directly from their wallets**, maintaining self-custody of their funds. If you want to understand the role of DEXs in the broader DeFi landscape, check our article on [decentralized applications (DApps)](https://dexkit.com/blog/the-power-of-decentralized-applications-dapps/).

**2\. Swaps on centralized exchanges (CEXs):** Many centralized exchanges offer instant swap services. You can exchange one cryptocurrency for another directly on the platform through a simple interface. It is quick and easy, though you might have to pay a higher fee and you temporarily give custody of your funds to the exchange. As we explain in [our CEX vs DEX comparison](https://dexkit.com/blog/cex-vs-dex-learn-why-you-need-both), CEXs are very convenient for fiat on/off-ramps but introduce custodial risk.

**3\. Atomic swaps:** These crypto swaps are also P2P transactions, **called “atomic” because they are executed in an all‑or‑nothing fashion**. Either the entire exchange happens or nothing happens at all, which prevents partial fills and many common fraud scenarios. Atomic swaps are usually executed via smart contracts and can even bridge assets across different blockchains.

### Why are crypto swap tools important?

Crypto swap tools play a vital role in the cryptocurrency ecosystem for several reasons:

* **Liquidity:** Easy conversion between cryptocurrencies enhances market liquidity and reduces slippage.
* **Portfolio diversification:** Swaps allow investors to rebalance and diversify quickly across multiple assets.
* **Arbitrage:** Swaps enable traders to capture price differences between markets or assets.
* **Hedging:** In a highly volatile market, swaps can be used to hedge exposure or move into stablecoins during high-risk periods.
