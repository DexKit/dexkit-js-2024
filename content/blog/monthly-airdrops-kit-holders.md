We’re thrilled to announce the launch of **monthly airdrops** for all eligible KIT holders! 🎉 Every month, a portion of the DexKit Polygon treasury is automatically distributed among our holders—no claim required.

### Why Monthly Airdrops?

Our goal is to reward long-term supporters of DexKit by sharing the value accumulated in our treasury, sourced from **Uniswap trading fees** and **service fees**. Monthly rewards vary dynamically based on treasury growth and community participation.

### How to Qualify

To take part in these monthly distributions, you simply need to:

- **Hold at least 1,000 KIT**  
- Maximum rewards are calculated based on holdings up to **10,000 KIT**  
- **Keep your KIT on any supported network** (Polygon, Ethereum, BNB Chain, Base, or Arbitrum.)

### Calculating Your Rewards

Use our built-in [Airdrop Calculator](https://dexkit.com/our-token/airdrop) to see exactly what you’ll earn each month.

![Rewards calculator](/blog-images/rewards-calc.png)

Just enter your KIT balance, and the calculator will estimate your **monthly** and **annual** rewards based on the current treasury size, KIT circulating supply, and market price.

### How It Works

1. **Treasury Growth**  
   Treasury increases through Uniswap trading fees and service revenue.
2. **Dynamic Calculation**  
   Rewards are proportionally distributed based on your share of circulating KIT at the time of distribution.
3. **Automatic Distribution**  
   Tokens are sent directly to your wallet—no manual claim needed and no gas fees.

### Getting Started

1. **Buy KIT** via our in-app “Buy KIT” button.  
2. **Hold at least 1,000 KIT** in any supported network.  
3. **Relax** and watch your rewards roll in every month!
