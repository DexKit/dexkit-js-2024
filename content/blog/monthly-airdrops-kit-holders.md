We’re thrilled to announce the launch of **monthly airdrops** for all eligible KIT holders! 🎉 Each month, **0.42% of our Polygon treasury** (equivalent to a 5% APR) will be distributed automatically among our community of holders—no claim required.

### Why Monthly Airdrops?

Our goal is to reward long-term supporters of DexKit by sharing the value we accumulate in our treasury. By tapping 0.42% monthly, we strike the perfect balance between sustainable growth and generous returns.

### How to Qualify

To take part in these monthly distributions, you simply need to:

- **Hold at least 1,000 KIT**  
- Maximum rewards cap out at **10,000 KIT**  
- **Keep your KIT on any supported network** (Polygon, Ethereum, BNB Chain, Base or Arbitrum.)  

### Calculating Your Rewards

Use our built-in [Airdrop Calculator](https://dexkit.com/our-token/airdrop) to see exactly what you’ll earn each month.

![Rewards calculator](/blog-images/rewards-calc.png)

Simply slide the “You Have” bar to enter your KIT balance and watch your projected monthly and annual rewards update in real time.

### How It Works Under the Hood

1. **End of Month Trigger**  
   A governance-controlled smart contract calculates 0.42% of the current treasury.  
2. **Proportional Distribution**  
   The pool is split among all holders based on their share of the total circulating supply (with a 1,000 KIT minimum).  
3. **Automatic Airdrop**  
   Tokens are sent directly to your wallet—no manual claim, no gas fees for claiming.

### Getting Started

1. **Buy KIT** via our in-app “Buy KIT” button.  
2. **Hold a minimum of 1,000 KIT** in any supported network.  
3. **Relax** and watch your rewards roll in at the end of each month!
