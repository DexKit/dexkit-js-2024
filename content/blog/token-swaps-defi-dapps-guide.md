## Common Pitfalls in Token Swap Integration

When integrating token swaps into your DApp, it's crucial to be aware of potential pitfalls that could hinder the process:

1. **Security Risks**: Ensure that your smart contracts are audited to prevent vulnerabilities that could be exploited by malicious actors.
2. **Liquidity Issues**: Without sufficient liquidity, users may face slippage or failed transactions, leading to a poor user experience.
3. **Regulatory Compliance**: Stay informed about the regulatory landscape to avoid legal issues that could arise from offering financial services.
4. **User Education**: Provide clear instructions and support to help users understand how to use the token swap feature effectively.

By addressing these challenges, you can ensure a smooth integration process and a positive experience for your users.
